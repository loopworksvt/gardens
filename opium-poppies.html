Care

Summarized from the sources listed below (web research, 2026-11-02). Where sources disagree, the page says so.

What helps it thrive

Full sun in well-drained soil enriched with organic matter before sowing. Poppies dislike transplanting, so sow in place and thin as needed.

Growing season

Sow anytime from autumn to early spring by scattering seed on the soil surface. A fall sowing after a killing frost keeps seed from germinating until spring. Most annual poppies germinate in late winter or early spring and survive spring freezes. Plants die after setting seed but often reseed.

Fall tasks

Direct-sow seed in fall after a killing frost (UVM Extension lists breadseed poppy among annuals to sow in fall).

Propagation

Seed only. Harvest when pods turn from green to tan, keep them upright, empty into a wide container and dry the seed at room temperature for several days. Deadhead if you do not want reseeding.

Dividing

Not applicable; annual grown from seed, and it dislikes transplanting.

Note

The search did not address legal restrictions on growing opium poppies, which vary by country and region; check local rules before growing or harvesting.
